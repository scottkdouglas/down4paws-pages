The 5 Rules of Recall

You'll learn the exact 5 rules that make recall stick.

  1. Never call your dog for something unpleasant.
  2. Only call when you're sure they'll come.
  3. Make it happen, every time.
  4. Say it once.
  5. Pay your dog what they're worth.

This system builds trust, confidence, and consistent listening. Have my free 5 Rules guide? The rules are the start. Total Recall is 30 days of practicing them, one small step at a time.
